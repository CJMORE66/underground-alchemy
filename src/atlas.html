01 / BREATHE & FIND THE POCKET +

Take an easy breath without forcing it. Speak one short phrase on an exhale, then leave space. At a comfortable pulse, put the same sentence before, on and after the beat. Stop if you feel uncomfortable.

02 / FOLLOW THE ASSOCIATION +

Start with a visible object. Say the next connected word, then another, for thirty seconds. If you freeze, name what you can see. Rhyming and polish can wait; keep an honest thread.

03 / BUILD A METAPHOR +

Give a feeling a material, a place and a sound. Replace “I feel stuck” with a concrete scene. Collect unfamiliar words from signs and books, check their meanings, then use one in a line of your own.

04 / LISTEN BACK WITHOUT A SCORE +

Listen once for a line worth keeping. Listen again for breath, repeated images and changes in energy. Write one question about what you heard. Lyrics can prompt self-reflection; they do not diagnose an emotional condition.
